About Matwana Routes Web

What is Matwana Routes Web?

Matwana Routes Web is your go-to platform for discovering and navigating matatu routes across the city. This is one is for the drivers and all those who appreciate art on their vehicles of commute. With this website, as a driver you'll be able to show off your ride and also add info on your routes so customers can find you in real life as well. You can also tell your customers if they can hire your ride and all that. All in all we want you to have fun as you hustle.

What Can You Use It For?

  • Register your routes and stops.
  • Get estimated travel times and fares.
  • Explore alternative routes to avoid traffic.
  • Stay updated with the latest route changes and news.
  • Connect with other drivers and passengers.
  • Share your experiences and tips with the community.
